## MODIFIED Requirements

### Requirement: Accurate public learning workflow

The system SHALL present the public homepage as a mature Morandi-purple and warm-white introduction to organizing independent study. It SHALL describe only the available course, chapter, question, error-reason, tag, learning-setting, and personal study-planning workflows. Illustrative labels SHALL use neutral preparation-oriented content rather than school-level example subjects. The homepage SHALL NOT use pink, green, or multicolor stationery-card styling, and SHALL NOT represent answer status, redo history, accuracy percentages, trend charts, progress analytics, reminders, recurring schedules, or external calendar synchronization as available product functionality.

#### Scenario: Visitor reviews supported features

- **WHEN** a visitor views the public homepage
- **THEN** the feature introduction describes only course, chapter, question, error-reason, tag, learning-setting, and personal study-planning workflows
- **AND THEN** no feature card or illustrative data claims that redo outcomes, accuracy trends, progress analytics, reminders, recurring schedules, or external calendar synchronization are available.
